INDUSTRIAL BEARING MAINTENANCE SOP
==================================

SYSTEM PURPOSE
This SOP provides maintenance guidance for industrial bearing fault conditions detected through vibration and multimodal condition monitoring.

The system identifies four operating conditions:
1. Normal Operating Condition
2. Inner Race Fault (IR007)
3. Ball / Rolling Element Fault (B007)
4. Outer Race Fault (OR007)


==================================================
1. INNER RACE FAULT (IR007)
==================================================

Fault Code:
IR007

Fault Description:
Damage or abnormal condition associated with the inner race of the bearing.

Severity:
HIGH

Possible Root Causes:
- Excessive radial load
- Shaft misalignment
- Improper press-fit mounting
- Inadequate lubrication
- Incorrect bearing installation

Typical Symptoms:
- Increased vibration
- Abnormal bearing noise
- Increased operating temperature
- Repetitive vibration pattern
- Progressive deterioration during operation

Inspection Procedure:
- Check shaft alignment.
- Measure shaft runout using a dial indicator.
- Inspect bearing mounting and press-fit condition.
- Check lubrication flow and lubricant condition.
- Inspect the bearing inner race for visible damage or wear.

Immediate Safety Action:
- Shut down the drive motor if vibration or temperature reaches unsafe levels.
- Prevent continued operation under severe fault conditions.
- Follow plant lockout/tagout procedures before physical inspection.

Corrective Maintenance:
- Correct shaft alignment.
- Restore proper lubrication.
- Correct improper bearing mounting.
- Replace the bearing if inner race damage is confirmed.

Preventive Maintenance:
- Perform periodic shaft alignment inspection.
- Monitor vibration trends.
- Maintain scheduled lubrication.
- Verify correct bearing installation during maintenance.


==================================================
2. BALL / ROLLING ELEMENT FAULT (B007)
==================================================

Fault Code:
B007

Fault Description:
Abnormal condition or damage associated with bearing balls or rolling elements.

Severity:
HIGH

Possible Root Causes:
- Particle contamination
- Electrical discharge fluting
- Lubricant breakdown
- Inadequate lubrication
- Excessive operating load

Typical Symptoms:
- Increased vibration
- Abnormal mechanical noise
- Increased bearing temperature
- Irregular vibration pattern
- Reduced bearing operating life

Inspection Procedure:
- Inspect lubricant for contamination.
- Check bearing housing for foreign particles.
- Inspect rolling elements for wear or surface damage.
- Check lubricant condition.
- Inspect for evidence of electrical discharge damage.
- Review recent vibration trends.

Immediate Safety Action:
- Stop operation if vibration becomes excessive.
- Isolate the machine before physical bearing inspection.
- Follow plant safety and lockout/tagout procedures.

Corrective Maintenance:
- Flush contaminated housing.
- Replace contaminated or degraded lubricant.
- Replace damaged rolling elements or bearing.
- Investigate and eliminate the source of contamination.

Preventive Maintenance:
- Maintain clean lubrication systems.
- Use appropriate lubricant.
- Monitor lubricant condition.
- Perform periodic vibration monitoring.
- Protect bearing assemblies from contamination.


==================================================
3. OUTER RACE FAULT (OR007)
==================================================

Fault Code:
OR007

Fault Description:
Damage or abnormal condition associated with the outer race of the bearing.

Severity:
HIGH

Possible Root Causes:
- Housing distortion
- Loose fitment
- Heavy external vibration
- Improper bearing installation
- Excessive mechanical loading

Typical Symptoms:
- Increased vibration
- Abnormal bearing noise
- Increased temperature
- Repetitive vibration pattern
- Progressive bearing deterioration

Inspection Procedure:
- Inspect housing bore dimensions.
- Check bearing fitment.
- Inspect housing for distortion or damage.
- Check mounting bolts and mechanical supports.
- Inspect surrounding equipment for excessive external vibration.
- Review vibration trend history.

Immediate Safety Action:
- Shut down the machine when vibration reaches unsafe levels.
- Isolate the equipment before inspection.
- Follow plant lockout/tagout procedures.

Corrective Maintenance:
- Correct loose bearing fitment.
- Repair or replace damaged housing components.
- Apply appropriate torque to mounting components.
- Install vibration-damping supports where required.
- Replace the bearing if outer race damage is confirmed.

Preventive Maintenance:
- Periodically inspect housing dimensions.
- Verify bearing fitment.
- Monitor external vibration sources.
- Check mounting torque during scheduled maintenance.
- Maintain vibration trend records.


==================================================
4. NORMAL OPERATING CONDITION
==================================================

Fault Code:
NORMAL

Fault Description:
Bearing operating within the expected monitored condition without a detected fault.

Severity:
LOW

Possible Root Causes:
- No abnormal bearing condition detected.

Typical Symptoms:
- Stable vibration
- Normal operating temperature
- No significant abnormal mechanical noise
- Stable operating condition

Inspection Procedure:
- Continue routine vibration monitoring.
- Check operating temperature periodically.
- Review historical vibration trends.
- Inspect lubrication condition according to maintenance schedule.

Immediate Safety Action:
- No emergency shutdown required when operating conditions remain within acceptable limits.

Corrective Maintenance:
- No corrective maintenance required unless abnormal trends are detected.

Preventive Maintenance:
- Perform routine monthly vibration checks.
- Maintain scheduled grease replenishment.
- Monitor vibration and temperature trends.
- Follow applicable plant maintenance procedures.
- Maintain historical maintenance records.


==================================================
GENERAL INDUSTRIAL MAINTENANCE GUIDELINES
==================================================

1. Always follow plant safety procedures before inspecting rotating machinery.

2. Use lockout/tagout procedures before performing physical maintenance.

3. Do not rely on a single sensor reading for critical maintenance decisions.

4. Compare current vibration readings with historical operating trends whenever available.

5. A high-confidence AI prediction should be treated as a diagnostic aid and should be verified through appropriate physical inspection.

6. If vibration, temperature, noise, or other operating conditions indicate severe deterioration, escalate the issue to the responsible maintenance engineer.

7. Record detected fault code, probable root cause, inspection findings, corrective action, and maintenance date for future analysis.

8. Replace damaged bearings according to plant maintenance procedures and equipment specifications.

9. Maintain clean lubrication systems and prevent contamination.

10. Use condition-based maintenance whenever sufficient sensor and historical data are available.
